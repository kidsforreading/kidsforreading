Blurb: Targeted for early readers, A Little Monkey and a Fish is a story about a young, playful, and clever monkey who learns to make his way in the world by mimicking his mother. Along his journey he is faced with new challenges to overcome and must decide whether or not to take some risks. What do you think he will do?
