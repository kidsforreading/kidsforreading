OUR INITIATIVE

Education Continuity & Combat Gender Bias

Geo-political risks, poverty, crime & economic hardships disproportionately affect a girl-child's education. We ensure free learning resources from Grade 1 to 5 (math, reading, speaking) in 32 languages.

TEACHER AND CAREGIVER RESOURCES INCLUDED

When education access is limited due to distance or restrictions our platform enables a teacher, care-giver, or parent to provide structured learning. UDL support ensures learning for disabled children.

ACHIEVING UN SDG VIA OPEN-SOURCE

Free, unrestricted, unlimited usage and sharing of the platform as an initiative to achieve United Nations Sustainable development Goals of quality education, reduce gender and overall inequality.

LOCALISED LEARNING

Our platform uses text-to-voice in local dialects to ensure ease of learning to read text and mastering speaking in younger years. UDL text to ensures friendly reading for vision impared children.
