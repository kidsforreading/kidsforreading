CONTRIBUTE TO THE PROJECT

We have classified contributors into three categories:

  • Augmentors: who donate their time to assist with translations, voice overs, or with the project.
  • Press and Media: who assist with distribution and for enhancing the reach of the project by creating press coverage. Please contact us at press@kidsforreading.com with your queries.
  • Institutions: those who enable us to improve the quality of the educational material(s). Please contact us at books@kidsforreading.com so that we may share our materials for your review.
